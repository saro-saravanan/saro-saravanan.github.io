How this works

Each member's share of the member allocation equals their patronage divided by total patronage. Qualified allocations are typically paid partly in cash and partly retained as equity in the member's name; non-qualified allocations are retained in full and handled differently on paper. Federal rules (Subchapter T) generally require at least 20% of a qualified written notice of allocation to be paid in cash ? this tool warns if you go below that, but it doesn't decide for you.

This is a calculator, not tax or legal advice. Your bylaws, state statute, and the co-op's CPA govern what's actually allowed and what gets reported. Use these numbers as a starting point for that conversation.
